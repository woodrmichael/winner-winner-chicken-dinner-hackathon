**Four minutes total, including up to two minutes live.** One teammate narrates while another operates the app. Aim for 1:45 live.

| Time | Show or say |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:35 | A family needs clothes and a ride after a fire. Vaun’s question for ComForce is whether help reached the family and which partner delivered it. |
| 0:35–2:20 | Follow the [referral walkthrough](mobile.md#check-a-complete-referral), skipping the reload. Start at two delivered services. Show the ride still outstanding after the coat delivery, then record the ride. End at four deliveries and name the provider. |
| 2:20–3:05 | Explain why Winter Care Collective matched both needs and how the resident chose the recipient and shared details. |
| 3:05–4:00 | Show the winter program’s second delivery alongside its funding. Close with what ComForce can now document for a partner or funder. |
